2. Gamemaker project titled "Suppression"

It's a simple game revolving around the idea of suppressing fire. Enemies spawn behind cover and shoot at you and attempt to move up. Your objective is to suppress them by firing at them, and kill them when they fire at you or attempt to move closer. The closer an enemy is, the greater their chances are of killing you.

4. Inter-VLAN routing topology
